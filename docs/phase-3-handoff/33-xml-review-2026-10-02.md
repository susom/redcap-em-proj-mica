## Verdict

**Not ready.** Four things are fixed since the 09-28 review:
- the `pre_screen` stop actions now really end the flow;
- `@TODAY` is off `randomization_date`;
- the Day-1 chain randomizes and hands the participant their session;
- the weekly SMS schedules correctly.

Four defects in the XML itself keep the trial from running as designed (C1, C2, C4, C5):
- minors and over-65s can consent and be randomized;
- the arm-2/3 public link skips consent;
- nobody is ever invited to the booster session;

Two more (C3, C6) apply if the target project copies 279's configuration:
- no reminder or weekly SMS ever sends, because the randomization date is never written;
- SMS alerts, including the phone-check passcode, are silently turned into emails to nobody.

**Prod check, same day (§6).** The source project is prod **PID 35968**, now titled "MICA-ED (R01)".
It is in **Production** status, so the C1/C2 fixes need Draft Mode or an admin. Its configuration is
better than 279's: **C3 and C6 do not apply on prod.** What prod adds:
- the CRC reviewer role has one member, the developer;
- all 22 alerts are active, and prod's queue shows the midnight sends;
- Enhanced SMS expires conversations at 180 minutes, not the PI's 1440;

- **Found while testing C4/C5 ([§8](#8-found-while-testing-the-module-stamps-randomization_date-before-randomization)):**

## 2. Findings

Each finding gives the evidence, the impact, the fix, and how it was verified. "Live" means it was

### Critical: the trial does not run as designed

**C1. Age is not an eligibility criterion. Minors and over-65s consent and are randomized.**
- **Evidence:**
  - `sc_age` has only a *soft* 18–65 range, so out-of-range values are allowed.
  - No logic anywhere reads `sc_age`.
  - `calc_screen_result` no longer exists.
  - `screen2`'s auto-continue checks only phone and pregnancy.
  - The randomization trigger is `tsr_complete=2` alone.
- **Live:** ages 17 and 70 both reached `consent` with the "You are eligible" banner. Nothing later
  stops them from reaching `tsr` and being randomized.
- **Fix:** restore the verified design ([ELIGIBILITY_CALC_PI_XML_2026-09-25](../screening/ELIGIBILITY_CALC_PI_XML_2026-09-25.md)),
  `pre_screen` → `auditc` → `screen2` → `screen_eligibility` → `consent`. All seven steps are needed.
  This exact set was applied to scratch PID 280 and tested (below).
  1. **Online Designer:** drag `screen_eligibility` to sit **after** `screen2`. In this XML it comes
     before `screen2`, so designating it alone would compute eligibility before the phone and pregnancy
     questions are asked.
  2. Add a calc field `calc_screen_result` (`@HIDDEN-SURVEY`) on `screen_eligibility`, using
     [`calc_screen_result.txt`](../screening/calc_screen_result.txt).
  3. **Branching:**
     - `desc_ineligible` → `[calc_screen_result] = '0'`
     - `desc_ineligible_hazard` → `[calc_screen_result] = '2'`
     - `consent.desc_eligible` → `[calc_screen_result] = '1'`, so the banner and the gate agree.
  4. Designate `screen_eligibility` on `day_1_ed_arm_1`.
  5. **Survey Settings, `screen2`:** keep auto-continue on, and **clear its condition**. Otherwise an
     AUDIT-C-negative person, whose `phone` is blank, still stops at `screen2` and never sees the
     message.
  6. **Survey Settings, `screen_eligibility`:** auto-continue condition `[calc_screen_result] = '1'`.
  7. **Randomization trigger:**
     `[day_1_ed_arm_1][calc_screen_result]='1' AND [day_1_ed_arm_1][consent_complete]='2' AND [day_1_ed_arm_1][tsr_complete]='2'`.
- **Tested on 280, 9/9 pass.** Every walk ended where it should, and the stored value matched the
  message on screen:

  | Case | Message | Stored | Ends |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | AUDIT-C-negative man | "not eligible" | 0 | thank-you |
  | never-drinker | "not eligible" | 0 | thank-you |
  | daily-cannabis woman | "not eligible" **and** the cannabis study | 0 | thank-you |
  | 17-year-old, 70-year-old, pregnant woman, man with no phone | resources | 2 | thank-you, no consent |
  | eligible man, eligible woman | "Congratulations! You are eligible" | 1 | `consent` |

- **Two nits remain, both the PI's call** (plus a third, found later on 280: an *eligible* participant
  now passes a `screen_eligibility` page with no visible text, because "You are eligible" sits on
  `consent` in this XML. Moving `desc_eligible` back onto `screen_eligibility`, as in the 09-25
  design, removes the empty page):
  - AUDIT-C-negative people still pass an empty `screen2` page first, because `phone` and `preg`
    are hidden for them.
  - The cannabis case reads "not eligible" and then "eligible for our cannabis study". The 09-25 doc
    has a branching that shows only the second.
- **Timing:** do this before the move to Production. The trigger and the designations lock there.

**C2. The arm-2 and arm-3 public links enrol people with no consent and no randomization.**
- **Evidence:** Day 1 of arms 2 and 3 still has `pre_screen`, `auditc`, `screen2`, `contact_info` and
  the baseline battery. `consent`, `person_obtaining_consent` and `check_code` are on arm 1 only.
  REDCap offers one public link per arm (`invite_participants.php:289`).
- **Live:** an eligible man screened through arm 2's link went `pre_screen` → `auditc` → `screen2` →
  **`contact_info`**. He never saw consent.
- **Fix:** untick the screening and baseline forms on Day 1 of arms 2 and 3. The list is in
  [30 § Before the move](30-go-live-readiness.md#before-the-move-or-it-needs-an-admin).
- **Timing:** designations lock in Production.

**C3. With 279's module settings, `randomization_date` is never written. That silently disables 13
alerts (02–14), the weekly-SMS ASI and every date calculation.**
- **Evidence:**
  - The field has no `@TODAY` and no calculation. Its label says it is "set by MICA EM".
  - 279 has no `stamp-randomization-date-field` row, and blank means off (`MICA.php:1206`).
- **What hangs off it:** alerts 02–14 anchor on it, the weekly-SMS ASI condition requires it, and so do
  `first_sunday_1200`, `calc_month_3/6/12` and `calc_esms_valid`.
- **Live:**
  - **Stamp off** (record 17, arm 2): no date, **zero** queued alerts.
  - **Stamp on** (record 18, arm 3): the date was written; alerts 02 and 04 queued, and the weekly SMS
    queued for Sunday 2026-10-04 17:00.
  - Alert 02 was then delivered to the mail sink. It had the correct name, and its link was the
    participant's own arm-3 ED session.
- **Fix:** MICA → Configure → "Stamp the randomization date into this field" = `randomization_date`.

**C4. The booster session is never offered. Alerts 05–14 can't queue, because nothing is ever saved at
Month 3.**
- **Status: fix built and tested on scratch PID 280, not on prod. See [§7](#7-c4-and-c5-fixed-and-tested-on-scratch-pid-280-2026-10-02-not-applied-to-prod).**
- **Evidence:** REDCap evaluates a logic-only alert only at the event being saved
  (`Alerts.php:141-352`; `getAlertsForInstrumentSave` and `evaluateLogic` use `$event_id`). Alerts 05–14
  require `[event-name]='month_3_arm_N'`, and nothing writes to a Month-3 event before the participant
  is invited.
- **Live:**
  - Record 18 (arm 3, randomized, date set) had no rows for 10/12/14.
  - One save at `month_3_arm_3` queued all three at once, for 2027-01-02, 01-08 and 01-15 (at 00:00).
  - So in production they fire only if something happens to be **saved** at a Month-3 event. Opening a
    form doesn't count.
- **Fix: evaluate the alerts in the Day-1 context, which is saved at randomization.** Per alert:
  - **Condition:** `[event-name]='day_1_ed_arm_2' and [month_3_arm_2][mica_booster_session_complete]<>'2' and …`
  - **Every** Month-3 token must carry the prefix, not only the link:
    - `[month_3_arm_2][survey-link:mica_booster_session]` in emails;
    - `[month_3_arm_2][survey-url:mica_booster_session]` in texts;
    - `[month_3_arm_2][form-link:close:…]` in 09/14;
    - `[day_1_ed_arm_2][study_group]`.
  - **What was verified:** from the Day-1 arm-2 event, `survey-link` and `survey-url` pipe to the
    Month-3 booster link. `form-link` was not tested.
  - Keep `ensure_logic_still_true=1`, so completing the booster still cancels the rest.
  - **Alternative:** an ASI on `mica_booster_session` at `month_3_arm_N`. REDCap evaluates every ASI
    on every save (`SurveyScheduler.php:2214`). But only one ASI is allowed per survey/event, so the
    email/SMS split would need a delivery-preference field.

**C5. No Month 3, 6 or 12 follow-up invitation exists.**
- **Status: 18 alerts built and tested on scratch PID 280, not on prod. See [§7](#7-c4-and-c5-fixed-and-tested-on-scratch-pid-280-2026-10-02-not-applied-to-prod).**
- **Evidence:** the only ASI is the weekly SMS. No alert sends an `auditc` link, and the module sends
  nothing at follow-up events (grep of `MICA.php`, `classes/`, `pages/`). `calc_month_3/6/12` say "FUP
  will be sent on that day at 10am", but nothing sends it. ASPIRE had two follow-up ASIs (email and SMS;
  [audit](../ALERTS_AUDIT_257_vs_262.md)).
- **Consequence:** alerts 18–20 ("follow-up done") can only fire after a follow-up the participant was
  never invited to.
- **Fix:** one sender per follow-up event, pointing at `auditc`, which starts the chain. Use the same
  Day-1-context pattern as C4, `[month_3_arm_1][survey-link:auditc]` with 91/182/365 days + 10 h. Or use
  ASIs with a delivery-preference field.
- **Timing:** needed before day 91 of the first participant.

**C6. With 279's Twilio scope, the phone-check passcode never arrives.**
- **Evidence:** 279 has `twilio_enabled=0` and `twilio_modules_enabled=SURVEYS`. An SMS alert on such a
  project is rewritten to EMAIL with no recipient (`Project.php:582`, `Alerts.php:1286`), so it goes
  nowhere and logs nothing. Alert 01 sends the passcode that `check_code.passcode` (required) asks for.
  Alerts 03/06/08/11/13 are SMS too.
- **On the source:** alert 01 did send today, so the source's Twilio works. This bites only if 279's
  configuration is copied.
- **Fix:** Project Setup → Twilio → "Surveys and Alerts". This setting doesn't travel in any export.

### High

**H1. An ineligible participant sees an empty page, then a blank "thank you" page.**
- **Evidence:** every ineligibility message (`desc_ineligible`, `desc_ineligible_hazard`, `cann_elig`)
  is on `screen_eligibility`, which is designated on **no event**. `screen2`'s fields only show when
  AUDIT-C is positive.
- **Live:** an AUDIT-C-negative man, a never-drinker and a daily-cannabis woman each saw a `screen2`
  page with no questions and a Submit button, then an empty acknowledgement. A pregnant woman and a man
  with no phone ended the same way. None was told to hand the tablet back.
- **Fix:** the full C1 fix, steps 1–6. Designating `screen_eligibility` alone is not enough. Tested on
  280: each of these cases now sees its message.

**H2. `close` is a blank page at Day 1 for every arm, and at Months 3/6/12 for arms 2 and 3. This is
the O1 fix regressing.**
- **Evidence:** `desc_baseline_end` branches on `[event-name]='baseline_arm_1'`, an event that doesn't
  exist. The three follow-up messages branch on `_arm_1` events only.
- **Live:** in all three Day-1 walks, `close` had **0 visible fields**.
- **Consequence:** the gift-card message ($20/$30/$40/$60) is never shown.
- **Fix:** rebranch on MICA's own events, as P8 did ([alerts README O1](../alerts/README.md)):
  - `desc_baseline_end` → `[event-name]='day_1_ed_arm_1' or [event-name]='day_1_ed_arm_2' or [event-name]='day_1_ed_arm_3'`
  - the Month 3, 6 and 12 messages the same way, across all three arms.

**H3. Every reminder goes out at midnight.**
- **Evidence:** alerts 02–14 anchor on a date-only field with a 0-hour lag.
- **Live:** queued `first_send_time` values were `2026-10-09 00:00:00`, `2026-10-16 00:00:00` and
  `2027-01-02 00:00:00`. On the source, alert 03 (an SMS) went out at 00:00:31 on 09-29.
- **Fix:** add a time offset to every time-lag alert, e.g. +10 h, which matches the "10am" in the
  `calc_month_*` labels. Texting at midnight is also outside normal quiet hours.

**H4. Senders and recipients are wrong.**
- **Sender:** every alert and the ASI send from `tramstudy@stanford.edu`, the previous study's inbox.
  Alert 02 tells participants "reply to this email".
- **Recipient:** 9 CRC alerts still go to the placeholder `micastudy@stanford.edu`.
- **Rebuild trap:** creating a project from this XML replaces every From address with the importer's
  own email (`ODM.php:2354`). That happened on 280.
- **Fix:** a real MICA study mailbox as sender, and the real CRC address. Re-check both after any
  rebuild.

**H5. User roles leak and over-grant.** Per `UserRights.php:4539`, `data_entry` 1 = View & Edit and
2 = Read Only; for export, 1 = Full Data Set.
- **"Read Only"** has View & Edit on every instrument.
- **"Export w/o Identifiers"** exports the **Full Data Set**, identifiers included, and has API export.
- **"Data Entry", "Read Only" and "Export w/o Identifiers"** have no access to `admin`, `contact_info` or
  `sms_code_check`. They also list five instruments that don't exist (`my_first_instrument`, `phq4`,
  `randomization`, `standard_care_resources_sc_arm`, `compensation`).
- **Fix:** rebuild these three roles from the current instrument list. Only "CRC (MICA reviewer)" and
  "Project Admins" are current.

**H6. 279's module settings, if copied as the reference, break four things.**
- **Reviewer role:** Reviewer, PI and Auditor are all mapped to "Project Admins" (one member). The CRC
  role is unmapped and empty, so a critical finding emails the admin, not the CRC. That breaks the PI's
  5-minute requirement ([31](31-critical-finding-crc-notify.md)). Role IDs are per project, so map by
  name on the target.
- **`chatbot_redcap_inject`:** set to `DDQ, AUDIT, BSCQ, SIP-2R`, which are display names. The module
  skips unknown names, so the Day-1 counselor gets **no** baseline data. Use `ddq, audit, bscq, sip2r`.
- **Enhanced SMS:** disabled, with **Disable incoming SMS = on**. With that, replies and STOP never
  reach REDCap, so `sms_stop` is never written. When incoming is on, ESMS writes `sms_stop___1`
  (`EnhancedSMSConversation.php:470`).
  - Its `phone-field-event-id` and `sms-opt-out-field-event-id` hold 279's event ID (1208); remap them
    to the target's `day_1_ed_arm_1`.
  - Expire Conversations = 1440 (the PI's figure) is set on 279; the reminder is 60 minutes.
- **`close-expired-sessions`:** off. A session the participant abandons is never safety-scanned.
  Switching it on closes such a session 24 h after the first message. **From the code, not run here:**
  closing sets `mica_ed_session_complete=2` (`SessionWindow.php`), so it would cancel alerts 02–04 for
  anyone who started chatting. That's a PI decision: scan abandoned sessions, or keep the day-7/day-14
  recovery for them.

### Medium

**M1. Alert 21 never fires from the SMS opt-out survey.**
- **Why:** it is evaluated only at Day-1 events, but `sms_opt_out` is saved at `weeks_112_arm_3`.
- **Live:** completing `sms_opt_out` didn't fire it; ticking `sms_stop` at Day 1 did.
- **Fix:** add a second alert gated on `[event-name]='weeks_112_arm_3' and [sms_opt_out_complete]='2'`.
  Note too that no sender for `sms_opt_out` exists, so it may simply be vestigial.

**M2. Alert 15 (the signed consent PDF to the participant) can miss.**
- **Why:** it triggers on the `person_obtaining_consent` save and needs `[email]`. `email` is collected
  on `contact_info`, which comes **after** consent. If the CRC uploads the PDF straight away, as alert 16
  tells them to, `email` is still blank. The alert fails and never re-evaluates.
- **Fix:** make it logic-only (no trigger form), so it fires on whichever Day-1 save makes both fields
  non-empty.
- **Related:** no e-Consent framework is on `consent`, so the PDF is made and uploaded by hand.

**M3. A wrong passcode doesn't stop anything.**
- **What happens:** `check_code` auto-continues unconditionally. "The passcode is incorrect" is only a
  message, so a participant with the wrong phone number carries on. Today the control is the CRC's
  manual check, which alert 17 asks for.
- **Don't** fix it with an auto-continue condition. Someone who mistypes would dead-end on a blank
  page with no retry, which is H1 again.
- **Don't** rely on "Hide Submit button" logic either. In 17.2.3 it is applied when the page renders
  (`Surveys/index.php:3375`), not as the participant types, so the button wouldn't come back once the
  code is right.
- **If a hard gate is wanted:** put the result on a second page of `check_code`, with a "go back"
  message. That would need testing first; it wasn't tried here.

**M4. The CRC's `admin` form always says "Not Yet Randomized", "Consent is not complete" and "Dummy
Email is blank".** Those three descriptives have no branching. Live: record 18, consented, randomized to
arm 3 and with a dummy email, shows all three.

**M5. Alert 17's text tells the CRC to set "Is the participant ready to be randomized?" on `admin`.** No
such field exists; randomization is automatic at `tsr`.

**M6. At Month 3, arm 2/3 participants rate the session before having it.**
- **Live:** `drug_use` → `postsession` (CEMI) → `mica_booster_session`. Form order puts `postsession`
  first, and the booster has no redirect.
- **Also:** because the booster ends there, `close` is never reached at Month 3 for arms 2/3, so alert
  18 can't fire for them.
- **Fix:** move `postsession` after `mica_booster_session` in form order, or give the booster an
  end-of-survey redirect to `[survey-url:postsession]`, as `mica_ed_session` has. Then let
  `postsession` continue to `close`.

**M7. The weekly-SMS feedback compares a weekly total with a per-occasion threshold.**
- **What happens:** `dquant` is "the total over the past 7 days", but `sd_*`/`bd_*` compare it with
  `calc_binge_threshold` (5 for men, 4 for women). A man who had 6 drinks all week is told it was "in
  the range considered unhealthy".
- **What else exists:** `gset` already uses the weekly limit, `calc_dquant_threshold` (14/7).
- **Decision:** clinical, so the PI's call. The spec script carries the same rule.

**M8. Alert 22 can't work.** It references `calc_eligible`, `drinksperweek` and `birth_sex`, none of
which exist. Its body is "TBD", and it would email `[email]`, which an ineligible person never gives.
`LogicTester::isValid` passes it, because it checks syntax, not fields. Delete it or rebuild it.

**M9. The 24-hour and 14-day survey time limits do nothing for these links.** REDCap counts the limit
only from an ASI or participant-list send (`Survey.php:3212`). The ED and booster links arrive through
`ed_session_url` and alerts.

**M10. The participant is asked "What is the patient MRN?" as a required question on `contact_info`.**

**M11. The two "I agree to the release of…" items are optional free text.** They cover substance use
(42 CFR Part 2) and mental health. Free text isn't a recorded yes/no. This is a question for the IRB.

## 8. Found while testing: the module stamps `randomization_date` before randomization

**Evidence (PID 280):**
- Record 30's first `pre_screen` submit (11:55:04) wrote `ed_session_url = …state=pending` **and**
  `randomization_date = 2026-10-02`. The date-derived calcs (`weekday`, `first_monday*`, …) then
  computed from it.
- Record 19, never randomized, has a date too.

**Cause:** for a record that isn't randomized yet, `redcap_save_record` writes the "pending" handoff
link (`MICA.php:912`). `writeSessionUrl()` stamps the date whenever the field is empty, whichever link
it's writing (`MICA.php:1206`). The intent in the code comment is the date of randomization.

**Effect on prod:** 35968 has the stamp on, so this is live there.
- Everyone who submits the pre-screen gets a "randomization date", including screen-fails and people
  stopped at `pre_screen`.
- For a participant randomized on a later day, the date is the screening day, so every alert
  anchored on it is early.
- Alerts are protected from unrandomized records only by their event or arm conditions. The C4/C5
  alerts carry the guard; 02–04 don't. A record created through an arm-2/3 public link (C2) would
  still get them.

**Fix (module code, not done here):** stamp only in the `RESOLVED` branch, when the real session link
is written. Existing wrong dates can't be told apart from real ones afterwards, except by
`study_group` being blank.
